## Defects, in the critic's words

### Blocking

- The chair is structurally incoherent: the tall back hoop and the lower arm loop are two concentric arcs with no visible joint to the seat rail or to each other, so the eye cannot resolve how the piece is built — it reads as a cradle or trolley frame, not a cafe chair. Redraw with an explicit back-post-to-seat-rail junction and arms that terminate into the posts.
- The table has no visible support. Its pedestal is entirely occluded by the chair except for an ambiguous blue smear through the back hoop, so the tabletop floats as a disc. Either shift the chair off-axis to expose the pedestal foot, or draw the column reading cleanly through the negative space of the chair back.
- The chair is dead-centre and bilaterally symmetric on a frontal axis, so there is zero spatial recession between chair and table — both objects sit on the same picture plane. Rotate to three-quarter view so the seat foreshortens and the overlap reads as depth.

### Major

- The background is flat green while grain and stipple appear only inside the illustration, so the artwork reads as a sticker pasted onto the surface rather than printed with it. Carry the same paper/riso texture across the full field at low opacity.
- Mixed viewpoints inside one object group: the plate is a flat top-down ellipse with concentric blue rings, but the tabletop it sits on is drawn in strong perspective. The plate needs the same foreshortening as the table ellipse.
- The saucer rim and the plate rim run tangent along nearly the same arc, fusing the two objects into one ambiguous blob. Separate them in plan or overlap them decisively — tangency is the worst of the three options.
- The blue contact shadow is a stippled ellipse far wider than the chair's actual footprint, reaching almost to both safe margins. It reads as noise rather than cast light and competes with the chair silhouette. Tighten it to the leg footprint and give it a directional bias.
- The shadow's lower edge crowds the chip row with almost no clearance, so stipple bleeds against the pill outlines and muddies their contour. Add a clear band of at least one chip-height between the artwork's lowest ink and the chip row.
- The bottle's base ellipse sits on the tabletop's rear edge with no contact shadow while the cup and plate both have one, so it appears to hover behind the table rather than stand on it.

### Minor

- Type is set larger and looser than it needs to be and the vertical rhythm is uneven: the amount crowds the baseline of 'Good Earth' while the gap from the amount down to the date line is visibly larger. Set consistent optical intervals down the stack and tighten the title's tracking.
- The minus in the amount is a hyphen-weight dash set too close to the rupee glyph; at display size it reads as part of the currency mark rather than a sign. Use an en-dash-weight minus with a hair space.
- The note preview truncates mid-word ('I c...'), which is a clipping artefact rather than designed truncation. Break at a word boundary or allow a second line.
- The two metadata chips have unequal internal padding relative to their icons — the dot in 'HDFC Savings' and the pin in 'Lodhi Colony' sit at different optical distances from the left cap. Normalise icon-to-label spacing and cap padding across both pills.
